The first week I lived in Leeds I locked myself out of my flat twice, which I'm told is some kind of record for our building. The second time it was raining and I was holding a bag of frozen peas from the Co-op on Hyde Park Road, because apparently that was the one thing I needed at nine at night. My flatmate Priya was on her shift at the cinema, so I sat on the steps with the peas going soft and texted her about every ten minutes. A guy from the ground floor, who I'd never spoken to, came out to put his recycling away and just sort of nodded at me and went back inside. Ten minutes later he came out again with a mug of tea. Didn't say much, just "you look like you need this." His name's Tomasz. We're friends now, sort of. I still have the mug, actually, which I should probably give back.
