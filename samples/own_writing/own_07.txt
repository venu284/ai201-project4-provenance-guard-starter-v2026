Went to the new ramen place on Mill Street, Kobo, on Saturday with Dev and Lila. Honest review: the broth is really good, properly rich, the tonkotsu especially, and they do a decent spicy miso. The noodles were a bit soft for me but Dev liked them. The egg was perfect, jammy in the middle. The problem is everything else. We waited 35 minutes for a table even though we'd booked, the music was so loud we were basically shouting, and it's £16 for a bowl, which is a lot when Ichiban down the road is £11 and not that much worse. The gyoza were nice but tiny, five for £6. I'd go back, but probably for a weekday lunch when it's quieter, and I'd skip the sides. Three and a half out of five.
