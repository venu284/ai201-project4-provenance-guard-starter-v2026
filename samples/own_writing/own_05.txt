I've been thinking about how I don't really call my grandma anymore. When I lived at home I'd go over to hers on Sundays and she'd make that rice with the burnt bit at the bottom that everyone fights over, and we'd watch some terrible quiz show while she shouted the wrong answers at the screen. Now I'm three hours away and it's always "I'll call her this weekend" and then the weekend goes. She doesn't text, she has a flip phone she refuses to replace. Last time I did ring she talked for forty minutes about her neighbour's new fence. I was kind of half listening while making pasta. Which is bad, I know. I don't think I'm a terrible grandchild, but I'm probably not a great one at the moment either. Going to try Sunday evenings.
