I think the library should stop letting people reserve the group study rooms for four hours at a time. Every time I go up to the second floor of Gould Library during midterms, the rooms are booked solid, and half of them have one person in them with a laptop and a bag of crisps, or nobody at all because they've gone to get lunch. Meanwhile there are actual groups of four or five people squeezed around the tables by the printers trying to finish a presentation. Two hours would be plenty. If you really need longer you could just book again. I get that some people like the quiet, but that's literally what the silent floor is for. It's not a huge deal in week three, but by week eight it honestly drives me a bit mad.
