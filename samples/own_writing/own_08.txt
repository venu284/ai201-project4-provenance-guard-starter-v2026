I take the bus to the university every day. The bus stop is near my flat. It comes at eight fifteen. Sometimes it is late. Then I wait in the cold. I usually sit at the back. I like to look out of the window. We go past a park and a big supermarket. There are many students on the bus. Some of them sleep. Some of them listen to music. I often read my notes for class. The ride takes about twenty minutes. Last week the bus broke down near the bridge. We all had to get off and walk. I was late for my first lecture. My teacher was kind about it. Now I leave ten minutes earlier. I do not want to be late again.
