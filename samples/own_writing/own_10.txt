I have a part-time job in a bookshop. I work there on Tuesdays and Saturdays. The shop is small and quiet. It is on a street near the station. My boss is called Mr. Hale. He is old and very friendly. He knows every book in the shop. My job is to help customers and put books on the shelves. I also clean the front window. Most customers are polite. Some just want to look around. A few ask me for books I do not know. Then I ask Mr. Hale. The pay is not very high. But I like the job a lot. I can read when the shop is empty. Last week I finished a novel at work. I learn new English words there too.
