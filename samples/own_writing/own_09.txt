Last Saturday I cooked dinner for my friends. I made chicken curry and rice. It is my mother's recipe. She sent it to me in a message. I went to the market in the morning. I bought chicken, onions, tomatoes and spices. The shop did not have fresh coriander, so I used dried. I started cooking at four o'clock. The kitchen got very hot. I cut the onions and my eyes hurt. My friend Ana came early to help me. She made a salad. At seven, everyone arrived. There were six of us at the small table. The curry was a little too spicy. Nobody complained. Ana ate two plates. After dinner we played cards. I was tired, but I was happy. I want to cook again next month.
