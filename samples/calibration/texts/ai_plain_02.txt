My name is Sam. I live in a small town with my mum and dad. We have a cat called Milo. He is grey and he likes to sleep. Every day I get up at seven. I eat my breakfast and go to school. My school is not far. I walk there with my friend Tom. We talk about games on the way. At school I like maths and art. I do not like to run in sport. After school I go home and do my work. Then I play with Milo. He chases a string. It is fun. We eat dinner at six. Mum cooks and Dad cleans up. At night I read in bed. Then I go to sleep. I like my life.
