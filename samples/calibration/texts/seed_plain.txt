I work at a small shop near my house. The job is okay. I start at nine and finish at five. My boss is a good man and he pays me on time. Sometimes it is very busy and I do not have time to eat. On those days I am tired when I get home. I make some rice and eggs, and then I call my sister. She lives in another city. We talk about our parents and about work. I want to save money and study again next year.
