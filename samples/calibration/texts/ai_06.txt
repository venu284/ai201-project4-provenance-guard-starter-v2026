Last year, I decided to start waking up at five in the morning, and honestly, it changed my life in ways I never expected. I used to be a dedicated night owl, convinced that my best ideas only came after midnight. But after months of feeling exhausted and scattered, I figured it was worth a try.

The first week was brutal. My alarm felt like a personal attack, and I spent most mornings staring blankly at my coffee. But slowly, something shifted. I started to notice the quiet of the early hours, the soft light creeping through the window, the rare feeling of having time entirely to myself. I began using that time to journal, stretch, and plan my day with intention.

What surprised me most was how much calmer I felt. Instead of rushing through my mornings in a frantic blur, I could move at my own pace. Small tasks no longer felt overwhelming, and I found myself more patient with the people around me.

Of course, it is not for everyone, and it took real discipline. But if you have been feeling stuck, I would encourage you to give it a try. You might be surprised by what you discover.
