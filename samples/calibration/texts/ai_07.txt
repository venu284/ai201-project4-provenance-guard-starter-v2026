I'm really sorry you're going through this, and I want you to know that what you're feeling is completely valid. Moving to a new city is one of the hardest transitions a person can face, and it's normal to feel lonely at first.

Here are a few things that might help. First, try to build small routines. Visit the same coffee shop each week, walk the same park trail, or join a regular class. Familiar faces gradually turn into friendly acquaintances, and acquaintances can become friends. Second, be proactive about reaching out. It can feel uncomfortable to take the first step, but most people are just as eager to connect as you are. Consider joining a local club or volunteering for a cause you care about.

Third, stay connected with the people back home. A weekly video call with old friends can provide a sense of stability while you build your new community. Finally, be patient with yourself. Friendships take time, and there is no deadline for feeling at home.

Remember that you are not alone in this, and that things will get better. I'm always here if you need someone to talk to.
