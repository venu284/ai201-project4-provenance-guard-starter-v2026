I'm really sorry you're going through this. What you're feeling is completely normal. Moving to a new city is one of the hardest things a person can do, and it's okay to feel lonely at first.

Here are a few things that might help. First, build small routines. Go to the same coffee shop each week, walk the same park trail, or join a regular class. Familiar faces slowly turn into friendly acquaintances, and acquaintances can become friends. Second, reach out. It can feel awkward to take the first step, but most people are just as keen to connect as you are. Think about joining a local club. Or volunteer for a cause you care about.

Third, stay in touch with people back home. A weekly video call with old friends can give you some stability while you build your new community. Finally, be patient with yourself. Friendships take time, and nobody's set a deadline for feeling at home.

You're not alone in this, and things will get better. I'm always here if you need someone to talk to.
