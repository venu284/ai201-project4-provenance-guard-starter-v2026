Our school has a garden. It is behind the main building. Every spring the students plant vegetables there. This year our class planted carrots, beans and tomatoes. We worked in groups of four. My group planted the beans.

First we dug the soil. It was hard work and our hands got dirty. Then we put the seeds in small holes. We gave them water every day. After two weeks the beans were growing. I was very happy to see them.

The teacher said that plants need sun and water to grow. She also said that gardens help the bees. I did not know this before. Now I want to have a garden at my house too. My grandmother has a small yard, so I will ask her. I think it is a good idea. Plants make the world beautiful and we can eat them too.
