Right so the funeral was a mess. Auntie Rosa turned up an hour late wearing, I kid you not, a bright yellow coat. Everybody stared. Dad pretended not to see. Then the vicar got grandad's name wrong, twice, and my cousin Dev snorted so hard he had to go outside.

I should've been sad. I was, later. In the car park, mostly, when somebody handed me grandad's old flat cap and it still smelled of that menthol tobacco he swore he'd quit. Stood there holding it like an idiot while the rain came down.

Funny thing is he'd have loved the yellow coat. He used to say black was for people with no imagination. Rosa knew that. Maybe that was the whole point and the rest of us were too wound up to notice.

Anyway we ate way too many sausage rolls after. Nobody talked about anything important. That's families for you, I guess.
