We went to the beach on Saturday. The day was hot and the sky was blue. Dad drove the car and Mum sat next to him. My sister and I sat in the back. We sang songs on the way. When we got there, we ran to the sand. The water was cold but nice. I swam with my sister. We found some shells and put them in a bucket. Mum made sandwiches and we ate them under a big umbrella. After lunch we built a sand castle. It was very big. Then a wave came and it fell down. We all laughed. In the evening we drove home. I was tired and I fell asleep in the car.
