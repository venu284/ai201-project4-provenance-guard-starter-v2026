I went to the shop today. It was small and old. I bought some bread and milk. The man at the till was nice. He said hello to me. Then I walked home. The sun was out and it was warm. I saw a dog in the park. It ran after a ball. I sat down for a while. I felt good. At home I made some tea. I ate the bread with jam. Then I watched a show on TV. It was a funny show. I laughed a lot. After that I read a book. It was about a boy and his dog. I liked it. Soon it was dark. I went to bed early. It was a good day. I hope tomorrow is the same.
