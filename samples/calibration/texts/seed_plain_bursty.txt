My mom works at the bank. She gets home late. When she comes in, she puts her bag on the chair, takes off her shoes, and asks me if I did my homework and if I ate something and if I fed the dog, and I say yes to all of it even when it is not true. Then we watch tv. The dog likes her best. I think it is because she gives him cheese when she thinks I am not looking, and he is always looking.
