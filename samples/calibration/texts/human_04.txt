I come from a small village in the north of my country. My family is not rich but we are happy. My father is a farmer and my mother is a teacher. I have two brothers and one sister. I am the oldest child.

I came to this city two years ago to study. At first it was very difficult for me. The language was hard and I did not know anybody. I was often alone in my room. But now I have some good friends from my class. We study together in the library and sometimes we cook food together on weekends.

I study computer science at the university. I like it because it is useful and I want to find a good job. After I finish, I want to work in a big company. Then I will send money to my family and build a better house for my parents. This is my dream. I will work hard to reach it.
