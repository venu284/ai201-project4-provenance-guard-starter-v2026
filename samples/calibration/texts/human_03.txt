Spent the whole weekend trying to fix the bike. Chain kept slipping, no idea why. Watched like four videos, bought the wrong tool, took it back, bought another wrong tool. Ridiculous. My neighbour Pete leaned over the fence about two o'clock Sunday and said, you've got the derailleur set up backwards, mate. Backwards! Three hours I'd been at it.

He fixed it in about ten minutes while I stood there holding a cold tea. Didn't even gloat much. Just wiped his hands on his jeans and said it happens.

Rode it round the block after. Felt stupid-happy, the way you do when a thing finally works. Wind in my face, a dog barking at me from somewhere, the whole lot. Might ride to work tomorrow if it doesn't rain. Probably will rain though. Always does when I make plans like that.

Anyway, owe Pete a beer now. Maybe two.
