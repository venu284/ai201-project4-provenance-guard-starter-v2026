In my opinion, public transport is very important for a city. Many people do not have a car, so they need buses and trains to go to work. If the bus is late, they are also late and the boss is angry.

The government should spend more money on transport. At the moment the buses are old and often broken. Also the tickets are expensive for students and old people. This is not fair. A good system helps everyone, not only people with money.

Another reason is the air. Cars make the air dirty and many children have problems with breathing. If more people use the train, the air will be cleaner. I think this is a strong reason.

In conclusion, I believe that the city must invest in public transport. It is good for workers, for poor people and for the environment. I hope the mayor will think about this.
